Hi to everyone who signed up for this work, and to all the newcomers. I want to clarify the situation that led to me damaging my relationship with the developers of the amazing Calamity Entropy mod.

I used 6ekoh’s works to display them in the background of my mod without permission — so that his works would remain in the mod. During the dialogue, he asked that his works not be displayed alongside the Calamity Entropy mod. Why? I don’t know — he has his own reasons. By such actions, a very important rule was violated — “Modder has no right to demand what mods players should load.”

I want to publicly apologize to the developers of the Calamity Entropy mod, to the subscribers who wanted to play with two active mods at once, and also to all of you. I am deeply to blame before all of you; I apologize once again for being foolish and naive, as well as for being poorly informed about the community rules.

The End Of A Calamity Menu Theme
Main menu theme. Calamitas, "Die With A Smile".

Client-side. Only the title screen. You can leave it on in multiplayer — nobody else needs the mod.

Features
- Custom art, logo, and menu look. Nothing in-game.
- Music player with a spectrum that follows the track (including your uploads). Freezes when paused. Hover to expand, collapse to a circle. Drag it by empty space; the circle stays with it. Position is saved.
- Play, pause, skip, seek, loop one track, shuffle.
- Upload your own songs from the player. MP3 and WAV convert to OGG (there's a progress bar). Click a custom track's artist name to rename it — tags are read on upload.
- Unplayable files get skipped and dumped into a broken folder so the mod still loads.
- Background gallery: follow the music, lock one scene, or shuffle every 0–15 seconds. Click scenes to pick the pool. 0 means it only changes when you leave a world.
- Logo gallery: pack logos (including layered Watermelono ones), plus uploads and other mods' menus.
- Drop PNG/JPEG logos and wallpapers into DieWithASmile/logo and DieWithASmile/wallpaper.
- Other-mod skies: use another enabled menu theme's title background (Wrath of the Gods included) or vanilla Forest. Cards show the tModLoader logo or that mod's icon.
- Edit Position: drag the logo, player, and menu buttons. Scroll to resize the logo. Pan uploaded wallpapers. Save keeps the layout.
- Accent colors in the galleries retint the player, hover, buttons, and UI icons (darker idle, lighter on hover).
- Don't Forget fades into a Deltarune-style heart scene. Come Along with Me fades into a letter scene with mouse-reactive hands.
- Gallery arts: Calamitas, Don't Forget, Come Along with Me, Last meeting, Yharim Never Die, Witch of Supreme Memories, Entropy of Freedom.
- Music fades when mods finish loading, when you enter a world, and when you come back to the menu.
- Theme, playlist, uploads, loop, shuffle, background, volume, and player position are remembered.
